How to: export, import, file
  1. Export from the bank. Open UKRSIB business: Виписки (log in first). Click Рахунок and tick every account, set Дата з to 1 January and Дата по to the last day of the quarter, press Сформувати, then the CSV icon above the table (not PDF, not XLS). One file holds all the accounts you ticked.
  2. Import here. Press Add UKRSIB statements (CSV) and choose the file. The calendar below then says Ready to file for a quarter the statements reach.
  3. Check. Open the quarter, look at the rows that are counted, and open Lines for the declaration.
  4. File in the tax cabinet. Open Електронний кабінет: Введення звітності (log in with your key first). Tick the declaration before and choose Додатково, Дублювати, or press Створити and pick form F0103309. Set the period, paste the lines with their Copy buttons, then Підписати and Відправити. The cabinet takes no file, so the lines are typed or pasted.
  5. Confirm and pay. The receipts appear in Перегляд звітності. Pay the single tax (line 14) and the military levy (line 25) from your bank by the pay day, with the quarter in the purpose ("за 3-й квартал 2026 року"): the calendar reads that to mark the quarter paid. Then press Mark as filed here.

What this report assumes
  • Group 3 single tax: 5% of income without VAT or 3% with VAT, and a 1% military levy, as sources read for {{ r.year }}. Rates and limits change: confirm them with the tax office or an accountant. This is not tax advice.
  • Income is money received from clients. Foreign currency counts in hryvnias at the National Bank rate of the day it reached the account; selling it later, and any rate difference, add nothing. The report uses the hryvnia amount the statement shows, so compare a receipt with the National Bank's rate to be sure of it.
  • The declaration is cumulative from 1 January: each quarter's tax is the total so far minus the declaration before (lines 13 and 24 of the form say so). The line numbers were read from a filed 2026 declaration; a later edition of the form may move them.
  • "Paid" is read from the words of your payments ("за 1-й квартал 2026 року"), so a payment without a quarter in its purpose is not set against one.
  • Nothing is filed, and the deadlines are not tracked here. The social contribution minimum is the monthly figure sources give for the year.
