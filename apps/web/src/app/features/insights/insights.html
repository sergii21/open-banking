How these numbers are counted
  • Only booked transactions count. Pending ones are left out until the bank books them.
  • Transfers between accounts and people are left out of income and spending.
  • Income is money in under “Income”. A refund reduces spending in its category instead of counting as income.
  • Each currency is added up on its own. Amounts in different currencies are never combined.
  • Standing payments are found in the last 13 months: the same payee, a similar amount, and a steady rhythm. They stop showing once a payment is overdue by more than half a cycle.
  • You can change a transaction’s category on the Transactions page; these figures follow.
