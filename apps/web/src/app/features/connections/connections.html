Your data

What we keep

  • Account names and masked numbers, balances, and transactions from your banks
  • The bank’s access tokens, encrypted at rest and never sent to your browser
  • A record of the calls shown in the API Inspector, in memory only

What we never see

  • Your bank login. You sign in on your bank’s own page
  • Full IBANs or account numbers; only a masked form is stored
  • The ability to move money without you approving it at your bank

Delete all my data

Withdraws access at every bank and erases everything we hold for this demo session, including payments.
